Protocol Information
DEWCON
The unified Atmospheric Moisture Assessment System. DEWCON monitors dewpoint conditions and activates the appropriate sub-protocol based on current readings.
JUICECON
Activates when dewpoint reaches 60°F or above. Monitors humidity severity on a descending scale (JC1 = worst). Designed by Joe Tritschler.
CCF — Cunningham Crackle Factor
Activates when dewpoint drops to 25°F or below. Monitors dryness severity on a descending scale (CCF1 = worst). Tracks static shock risk, skin crackle potential, and general atmospheric desiccation. Designed by Sheryl Cunningham.
